Our story

Legal excellence, delivered with commercial sense

Ashworth & Grey was founded in 2008 with a simple conviction: that businesses deserve legal advice that is both technically excellent and genuinely useful. Too often, legal counsel is delivered in language that obscures rather than clarifies. We set out to change that.

Today we advise companies, investors, and institutions on their most consequential legal matters — from acquisitions and disposals, to complex disputes and regulatory investigations. Our clients range from ambitious UK SMEs to multinational corporations with operations across several continents.

We remain deliberately mid‑sized. It allows us to give every engagement the attention of a partner and to keep our fees transparent and predictable.

Our values

Integrity first

We give our honest view, even when it is not what the client hoped to hear. Our independence is the foundation of our advice.

Commercial pragmatism

Legal advice must serve the business objective. We weigh risk against opportunity, cost against outcome, and find the shortest safe path.

Long‑term partnership

We measure success by the decades, not the deal. Most of our clients have worked with us for over five years.
