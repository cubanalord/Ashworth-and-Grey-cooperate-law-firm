Corporate Law

End‑to‑end counsel for companies at every stage of growth — from incorporation to exit.

Our corporate team advises founders, boards, investors, and financial institutions on the transactions and governance questions that define a business. We are known for drafting that holds up, negotiation that moves fast, and advice that directors can act on.

  • Mergers & acquisitions
  • Corporate structuring & reorganisation
  • Shareholder & partnership agreements
  • Joint ventures & strategic alliances
  • Private equity & venture capital
  • Corporate governance advice
  • Commercial contracts
  • Cross‑border transactions

Strategic advocacy in the Commercial Court, arbitration tribunals, and mediation rooms.

Regulatory & Compliance

Helping regulated businesses stay ahead of the rules — and respond decisively when regulators come knocking.

  • Financial services regulation
  • Data protection & GDPR
  • Anti‑bribery & AML
  • Regulatory investigations
  • Compliance programme design
  • Internal investigations
  • Sanctions advice
